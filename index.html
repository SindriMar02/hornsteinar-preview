The firm maintains a healthy balance of public and private clients. Participation in design competitions has been an important component in the office’s organization and philosophy, offering a welcoming opportunity to release and execute new ideas, while learning from the past as well as looking to the future.

To create good architecture is to make the design process a creative dialogue, bringing different interests and parties together in a development of an idea from the first analysis to the finished project. The firm has received numerous prizes and awards in recent years- and many of its most interesting projects result from successful entries in architectural competitions

Diverse and complicated projects have called for a well organized and highly focused approach to solutions, collaborating with users as well as authorities, other specialists and different parties of interest. Communicating and presenting ideas to the public and/or prospective users in a simple but effective way is an important element of the firm’s working methodology and has contributed to its success.
